About me

I'm Tshwjxeeb Vaaj.

Hi, I'm Tshwjxeeb, a learning designer with a background in chemistry education, program leadership and coaching. I hold a degree in chemistry, a master's in chemistry education and a teaching credential in chemistry.

I help people build skills they can use right away by designing practice that feels like the real moment.

My background

From the court to the training room.

As program director and head coach of a boys' volleyball program, I designed learning well beyond the classroom. In the classroom, I taught chemistry curriculum aligned to the Next Generation Science Standards (NGSS) adopted by California. On the court, I taught motor skills, breaking complex movements into steps athletes could practice and master. I worked with learners from kindergarten through high school graduation, and I trained and mentored the coaches on my staff so they could lead on their own with confidence.

Instructional design brings both sides of my experience together. I understand how learning works from my time in the classroom and my graduate study, and I've applied Universal Design for Learning (UDL) principles on the court as well as in a lab. That foundation lets me partner with subject matter experts in any field: they bring the expertise, and I bring the strategies and tools that turn it into training people can actually apply. I'm a creative at heart, and I'm excited to build learning that helps teams and organizations perform. Let's connect and explore what we can create together.

More about me

Life off the clock.

Outside of work, you'll find me deep in an RPG or a cozy game, tinkering with retro handhelds, or building yet another mechanical keyboard.

Volleyball is still part of my life, and I love getting out into nature and traveling whenever I can.

I believe the best learning happens when people feel safe to try, miss, and try again. That guides me professionally as much as it does personally.
