About me

I'm Tshwjxeeb Vaaj.

Hi, I'm Tshwjxeeb, a learning designer with five years of designing and leading training as a program director and head coach, and a background in chemistry education.

I help people build skills they can use right away by designing practice that feels like the real moment.

My background

From the court to the training room.

As program director and head coach of a boys' volleyball program, I designed learning well beyond the classroom. I've taught chemistry to high schoolers and motor skills to athletes, and mentored both the coaches on my staff and a 2nd grade student teacher. My learners have ranged from kindergarten through high school graduation, plus the adults who teach them.

Instructional design brings both sides of my experience together. I understand how learning works from my time in the classroom and my graduate study, and I've applied Universal Design for Learning (UDL) on the court as well as in the lab. That foundation lets me partner with subject matter experts in any field: they bring the expertise, and I bring the strategies and tools that turn it into training people can actually apply. I'm a creative at heart, and I'm excited to build learning that helps teams and organizations perform. Let's connect and explore what we can create together.
