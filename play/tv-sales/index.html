    New associate onboarding · Lesson 1

    Display Tech Essentials

    Learn the four display technologies on our floor, the five specs customers ask about, and a simple flow for every sale.

    • ⏱ 25 min
    • ▤ 10 sections
    • ✓ Pre-check, 5 practice scenarios + exit assessment

    Section 1

    What makes a great TV picture?

    Customers rarely ask about nits or dimming zones. They ask, “Will it look good in my room?” Your job is to translate specs into their room, their content and the people watching.

    In this lesson you'll preview the four technologies on our floor, go deeper on where each one wins, then practice with real customer scenarios.

    Knowledge check · What do you already know?

    One question for each TV on our floor

    Four quick questions, one at a time. They aren't graded and you don't need to get them right to move on — answer with your best guess, read the feedback, then continue.

    Section 2

    The 5 specs customers ask about

    Select each spec to expand it. Read one at a time — you don't need to memorize numbers.

    Section 3

    Four technologies on our floor

    Same scene on all four screens: a bright star in a night sky. Notice four things: how black the sky is, how bright the star is, how vivid the colors are, and how much glow spreads around the light. Then select + to see how each one makes light.

    Section 4 · Deep dive

    Four panel technologies — and the room each one wins

    Now go deeper. Each tab answers the same questions in the same order — including how it fits a bedroom: low light, off-axis viewing and nighttime eye strain.

    Section 5 · Quick guide

    Quick scenario guide: guess, then reveal

    Read each customer type and decide which TV you'd recommend. Then select + to check yourself.

    Section 6 · The sale

    The sale in three moments

    Select a tab to see what to do before, during and after the sale.

    Practice · Simulation

    Your turn on the floor: 5 customers

    Read each customer, choose a recommendation and submit. A wrong answer explains why — take it again until you get it right.

    1. Read the customer
    2. Pick A, B or C
    3. Submit
    4. Read your decision results

    Exit assessment

    Show what you learned

    Ten questions covering the whole lesson, one at a time. Score 80% or higher to pass. You'll see your results by topic and how far you've come since the pre-check.
