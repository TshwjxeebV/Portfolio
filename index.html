My approach · Backward design

Built from real moments, not theory.

Every module starts with a real moment on the job, the court or in class. Then I design it backward, in three steps.

  1. 01Start with what they must doThe real action on the job, the court or in class.
  2. 02Decide the proofHow I'll know they can do it, before I build anything.
  3. 03Build the practiceScenarios and games that rehearse that exact action.
